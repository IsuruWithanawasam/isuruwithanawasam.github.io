About

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Imaging Physics at Delft University of Technology (TU Delft), the Netherlands, working in biomedical optics and computational imaging. My research focuses on optical coherence tomography (OCT): developing high-resolution OCT systems, together with the reconstruction methods behind them, to support non-invasive skin cancer diagnostics.

Before joining TU Delft, I completed the Erasmus Mundus LASCALA Master's programme across Lund University, the University of Szeged and Paris-Saclay University. My Master's thesis at Lund applied Gas in Scattering Media Absorption Spectroscopy (GASMAS) to lung monitoring. I hold a BSc (Hons) in Physics from the University of Colombo, Sri Lanka.

More broadly, I enjoy building light-based instruments that solve practical problems, from non-invasive medical imaging to environmental sensing.
